Timing boundaries and run exclusions

Timed work

Whole-process time includes launch, input reading or imports, computation, writes and exit. An internal timer covers only its delimited stages, and a kernel experiment covers the stated grouping, packing and scoring operations.

Observed variation

Short-run stage times are nonmonotonic and include shared-VM outliers. An early timeout-wait pilot biased process timing, so the entire pilot was excluded for all variants.

Scope of the optimization studies

The studies measure execution time and agreement with a retained computational contract. They do not test profitability, predictive generalization, data quality across markets or live trading performance.
