Separate reuse from training speed

A sweep speedup can reflect shared training statistics, fewer fits, or shared backend startup. A single-fit comparison answers a different question. Record which phases each timer includes and keep model settings, evaluation, and requested trials matched.

When evaluating a runtime claim, check the train and validation shapes, unique fit count, hardware, and backend versions. Time measured at a smaller scale can support a projection, which should be identified separately from measured results.

The historical benchmark reports are retained in the research record with their original workloads and timing boundaries.
