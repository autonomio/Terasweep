Reuse on the 1,014-row grid

Enabling reuse of statistics, fitted predictions and scores reduced the Rust internal time from 511.384 to 4.440 ms, a ratio of 115.18. The prepared input and Rust scoring and output conventions were held fixed.

The native CLI’s full-process median was 31.280 s, compared with 28.887 ms for the original cached Rust process, a ratio of about 1,083. Preparation, orchestration, metrics and artifacts differ across those two processes.

Remaining work on the grid

Fit and path counts

The 1,014 rows use six model fits and six scoring paths. With one scoring job per model, most SIMD lanes are unoccupied, and startup and scheduling dominate the remaining differences.

Each native model also produced 1,430 positive predictions in 1,431 test rows. The grid therefore contains little variation in scoring paths.

The million-row process timings

On the original 55-feature workload, the complete-process median fell from 6.942 s to 0.758 s across five stages, a ratio of 9.16 to the original Rust runner on that host. The separate paired assembly comparison measured 0.740 s under its own protocol.
