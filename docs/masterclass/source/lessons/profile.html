The two constant patterns account for 93.1% of the first million rows’ valid score misses. For a finite prediction vector, the scorer retains the extrema of the predictions and of their absolute values. Under the rule p > t, max(p) ≤ t implies all-off and min(p) > t implies all-on. Inclusive rules place equality on the other side of each boundary.
